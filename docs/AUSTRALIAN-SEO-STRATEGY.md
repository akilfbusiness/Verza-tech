### Regional Content Optimizations

#### Australian Spelling and Terminology
- ✅ Use "organisation" not "organization" in AU content
- ✅ Use "analyse" not "analyze"
- ✅ Use "optimise" not "optimize"
- ✅ Use "centre" not "center"
- ✅ Use "colour" not "color"
- ✅ Reference AUD pricing prominently

#### Time and Date Formats
- Use DD/MM/YYYY format in Australian content
- Reference AEST/AEDT timezones
- Mention "business days" as Monday-Friday
